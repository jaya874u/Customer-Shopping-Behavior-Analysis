# 🛍️ Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing customer shopping behavior and sales transaction data to identify meaningful patterns in customer preferences, purchasing behavior, product performance, revenue generation, customer loyalty, discounts, shipping methods, subscriptions, purchase frequency, and geographic performance.

The project combines *SQL analysis* with an interactive *Power BI dashboard* to transform raw customer data into actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall customer and revenue performance
- Understand customer segmentation and loyalty
- Identify top-performing products and categories
- Compare revenue across customer demographics
- Analyze customer purchasing and spending behavior
- Evaluate discount and shipping patterns
- Analyze payment method preferences
- Identify high-value and repeat customers
- Generate actionable insights for data-driven decision-making

## 🔍 Key Insights

### 👥 Customer Segmentation

- Loyal customers represent approximately *79.9% of customer segments*.
- Returning customers account for approximately *17.9%*.
- The high proportion of loyal customers indicates strong repeat-purchase behavior and customer retention potential.

### 💰 Revenue & Sales

- The analysis generated approximately *$233K in total revenue*.
- The average order value is approximately *$60.48*.
- Revenue performance varies across customer segments, demographics, categories, and locations.

### 🏷️ Discount Analysis

- Discount usage varies across customers and products.
- Some products have significantly higher discount rates than others.
- Discount patterns can be used to develop targeted promotional strategies.

### 🚚 Shipping Analysis

- Express shipping has a slightly higher average order value compared with Standard shipping.
- Shipping preferences can help businesses optimize delivery options and customer experience.

### ⭐ Product Performance

- Gloves have the highest average product rating among the analyzed products.
- Product ratings can help identify products with strong customer satisfaction.

### 🌍 Geographic Performance

- Revenue varies significantly across different locations.
- Among the analyzed top-performing locations, *Montana records the highest revenue, at approximately *$5.8K**.

### 💳 Payment & Purchase Behavior

- Customer payment preferences provide useful information for improving the checkout experience.
- Purchase frequency and spending behavior can help identify high-value customer groups.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, businesses can:

1. *Focus on loyal customers* through personalized offers and loyalty programs.
2. *Target high-value customers* with customized marketing campaigns.
3. *Optimize discount strategies* instead of offering discounts uniformly.
4. *Improve product performance* by focusing on highly rated and high-demand products.
5. *Use geographic insights* to identify high-performing and growth-potential markets.
6. *Optimize shipping options* based on customer preferences and order value.
7. *Use customer segmentation* to create more effective marketing strategies.
