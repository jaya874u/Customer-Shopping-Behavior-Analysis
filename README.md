Customer Shopping Behavior Analysis
📊 Power BI & SQL Data Analytics Project
From Customer Data to Business Decisions.
🔎 Project Overview
This project analyzes customer shopping behavior and sales transaction data to identify meaningful patterns in customer preferences, product performance, revenue generation, customer loyalty, discounts, shipping methods, subscriptions, purchase frequency, and geographic performance.
The project combines SQL analysis with an interactive Power BI dashboard to transform raw customer data into actionable business insights.
The main objective is to understand who the customers are, what they purchase, how they purchase, and which factors influence revenue and customer retention.
🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze overall customer and revenue performance
Understand customer segmentation and loyalty
Identify top-performing products and categories
Analyze revenue across gender, age groups, seasons, and locations
Compare Standard and Express shipping performance
Analyze the impact of discounts on purchasing behavior
Compare subscribers and non-subscribers
Identify repeat buyers and high-value customers
Analyze payment method preferences
Study purchase frequency and average order value
Identify opportunities for improving customer retention and revenue

💡 Key Business Insights
Customer Segmentation
Loyal customers represent approximately 79.9% of the customer segments, followed by returning customers at 17.97%. This indicates strong repeat-purchase behavior and customer retention.
Product Performance
Among the top five products by average review rating, Gloves have the highest rating at approximately 3.9.
Discount Analysis
Hat has the highest discount rate shown in the analysis, at approximately 50%.
Shipping Analysis
The average order value for Express Shipping (60.48) is slightly higher than Standard Shipping (58.46).
Geographic Performance
Among the top 10 locations shown in the dashboard, Montana records the highest revenue at approximately 5.8K.
